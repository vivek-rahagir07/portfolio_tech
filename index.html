My Journey

From writing my first broken lines of code to architecting systems that scale.

Phase 01
Seeing, Failing & Adapting
The Beginning
Jul '23 – Dec '23

Started my journey with nothing but a laptop and a drive to build. The first months were humbling—seeing what great engineers were building online and feeling miles away from it. I faced constant failures, wrote terrible code, and had zero clients. But each failure was a lesson. I kept showing up, kept building small broken things, and slowly started to understand what good engineering actually looks like.

Freelancing HTML/CSS JavaScript Self-taught Resilience
Phase 02
The Learning Phase
Diving Deep into Technology
Jan '24 – Dec '24

With persistence came curiosity. I dove head-first into learning—exploring IoT hardware protocols, reading firmware documentation, watching electronics tear-downs, and building small experiments just to understand how things work. I explored embedded systems with C++, tinkered with microcontrollers, and began understanding how physical hardware communicates with the digital world. This raw, self-directed exploration planted the seeds for everything that would come next.

IoT C++ Microcontrollers React Node.js Embedded Systems

Focusing on Efficiency
Finding My True Calling
Jan '25 – Aug '25

I realized that just trying to force traditional freelancing wasn't working. So I slowly shifted my focus entirely toward my genuine technical interests. I stopped chasing clients and started building highly efficient, purpose-driven projects. This inward focus ignited a deep passion for system optimization and complex problem-solving, acting as a major turning point in my career.

System Design Optimization Full Stack WebSockets Redis
