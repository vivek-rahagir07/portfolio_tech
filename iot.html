IoT & Robotics

My Passion for Hardware

Although my primary career path is in software development, IoT and robotics have always been a passion of mine. Over the years, I've worked with more than 10 different microcontrollers and processors, including Raspberry Pi, ESP32, ESP8266, STM32, Arduino, and others.

I've built a variety of IoT and robotics projects that have strengthened my understanding of embedded systems, sensors, communication protocols, and real-world automation. What fascinates me most about this field is its vast scope and the endless opportunities to innovate.

I enjoy exploring new hardware, experimenting with emerging technologies, and continuously expanding my knowledge. Regardless of where my career takes me, IoT and robotics will always remain a field I'll keep learning, building, and contributing to.
